## Notes

- Earthrise was captured on 2026-04-06 from a camera mounted on an Orion solar
  array wing during the Artemis II crew's lunar flyby.
- The Full Moon image was produced from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter data.
- The Mars globe is a global color view assembled from Viking mission
  observations.

## Processing

Each original was downloaded from `images-assets.nasa.gov` and resized with
high-quality bicubic interpolation, keeping the original aspect ratio and
without cropping. The resized copies are re-encoded as JPEG (quality 82–86) to
keep page weight low.
